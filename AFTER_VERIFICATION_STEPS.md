## ✅ **Immediate Next Steps (Do These Now!)**

### **Step 1: Submit Your Sitemap**

This tells Google about all your pages.

1. In Google Search Console, click **"Sitemaps"** (left sidebar)
2. Click **"Add a new sitemap"**
3. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
4. Click **"Submit"**

You should see:
```
✓ Sitemap submitted successfully
  Status: Success
  Discovered URLs: [number]
```

---

### **Step 2: Request Indexing for Key Pages**

Tell Google to index your important pages immediately.

1. Click **"URL Inspection"** (left sidebar)
2. Enter these URLs one by one:

   - `https://stock.incomegrow.in`
   - `https://stock.incomegrow.in/stocks`
   - `https://stock.incomegrow.in/stocks/gainers`
   - `https://stock.incomegrow.in/stocks/losers`

3. Click **"Request Indexing"** for each
4. Wait for confirmation

---

### **Step 3: Check Coverage**

1. Click **"Coverage"** (left sidebar)
2. Check for any errors
3. Fix any issues that appear
